Landing
=======

.. requirement:: MSN-001
   :verification: Test

   The mission system shall land the crew within 200 m of the planned landing site, with the lunar module intact for ascent.

.. requirement:: MSN-002
   :verification: Test

   The mission system shall be able to abort the landing at any time before touchdown and climb back towards orbit.

.. requirement:: MSN-003
   :verification: Demonstration

   The crew shall be able to monitor the descent from the cockpit and take over its final phase.

.. requirement:: MSN-004
   :verification: Test

   The crew shall be alerted to every failure that needs their action, until they acknowledge it.

.. requirement:: MSN-005
   :verification: Analysis

   The landing shall take place with the sun between 7° and 20° above the eastern horizon at the landing site.
