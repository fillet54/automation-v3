Guidance and control
====================

.. requirement:: LM-GNC-001
   :satisfies: MSN-001, MSN-003
   :subsystem: GNC
   :verification: Test
   :last-modified-by: doors-import

   The guidance computer shall fly the descent from powered descent initiation to touchdown, the crew able to take over its final phase.

.. requirement:: LM-GNC-002
   :satisfies: MSN-003
   :subsystem: GNC
   :verification: Demonstration
   :last-modified-by: doors-import

   The crew shall monitor and command the guidance computer through its display and keyboard (DSKY).

.. requirement:: LM-GNC-003
   :satisfies: MSN-001, MSN-011
   :subsystem: GNC
   :verification: Analysis
   :last-modified-by: doors-import

   The guidance computer shall take each mission's numbers from an erasable load, loaded before flight.
