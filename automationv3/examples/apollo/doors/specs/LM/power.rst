Electrical power
================

.. requirement:: LM-EPS-001
   :satisfies: MSN-011
   :subsystem: EPS
   :verification: Inspection
   :last-modified-by: doors-import

   The standard lunar module shall carry four descent batteries of 415 Ah each.

.. requirement:: LM-EPS-002
   :satisfies: MSN-004
   :subsystem: EPS
   :verification: Test
   :last-modified-by: doors-import

   The electrical power system shall light the BATTERY caution on a battery fault or a bus below 26.5 V, and the WARNING light on a bus below 24 V.
