Landing
=======

.. requirement:: LM-LDG-001
   :satisfies: MSN-001
   :subsystem: LDG
   :verification: Test
   :last-modified-by: doors-import

   The landing gear shall take a touchdown at up to 3.05 m/s vertically and 1.22 m/s horizontally.

.. requirement:: LM-LDG-002
   :satisfies: MSN-001
   :subsystem: LDG
   :verification: Test
   :last-modified-by: doors-import

   The probes under the footpads shall light the LUNAR CONTACT light 1.7 m above the surface.

.. requirement:: LM-LDG-003
   :satisfies: MSN-001
   :subsystem: LDG
   :verification: Test
   :last-modified-by: doors-import

   The landing radar shall report good altitude data below 12,000 m, and good velocity data below 7,500 m.

.. requirement:: LM-ABT-001
   :satisfies: MSN-002
   :subsystem: ABT
   :verification: Test
   :last-modified-by: doors-import

   ABORT STAGE shall separate the ascent stage and fire the ascent engine, only with MASTER ARM on.

.. requirement:: LM-ABT-002
   :satisfies: MSN-002
   :subsystem: ABT
   :verification: Test
   :last-modified-by: doors-import

   ENGINE STOP shall shut the descent engine down, whatever the guidance computer commands, for as long as it is pressed.
