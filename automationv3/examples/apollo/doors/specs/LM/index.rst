Lunar Module Specification
==========================

The lunar module carries two crew from lunar orbit to the surface and
back. Its descent stage holds the descent engine, its propellant, the
landing gear, and most of the batteries, oxygen and water for the stay;
its ascent stage holds the crew, the guidance computer and the ascent
engine. Each requirement here traces up to the mission requirements.
