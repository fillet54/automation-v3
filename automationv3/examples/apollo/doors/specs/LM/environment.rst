Environmental control
=====================

.. requirement:: LM-ECS-001
   :satisfies: MSN-011
   :subsystem: ECS
   :verification: Inspection
   :last-modified-by: doors-import

   The standard lunar module shall carry 22 kg of oxygen and 150 kg of water in its descent stage.

.. requirement:: LM-ECS-002
   :satisfies: MSN-004, MSN-010
   :subsystem: ECS
   :verification: Test
   :last-modified-by: doors-import

   The environmental control system shall light the ECS caution when less than 10% of the oxygen or the water remains.
