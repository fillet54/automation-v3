Descent propulsion
==================

.. requirement:: LM-PRP-001
   :satisfies: MSN-001
   :subsystem: PRP
   :verification: Test
   :last-modified-by: doors-import

   The descent engine shall throttle from 10% to 60% of its maximum thrust of 45,040 N, and run at a fixed full-throttle position of 92.5%; it shall never run between 60% and 92.5%.

.. requirement:: LM-PRP-002
   :satisfies: MSN-011
   :subsystem: PRP
   :verification: Inspection
   :last-modified-by: doors-import

   The standard lunar module shall carry 8,200 kg of descent propellant.

.. requirement:: LM-PRP-003
   :satisfies: MSN-001, MSN-004
   :subsystem: PRP
   :verification: Test
   :last-modified-by: doors-import

   The descent tanks' low-level sensor shall light the DES QTY caution when 5.6% of the propellant remains.
