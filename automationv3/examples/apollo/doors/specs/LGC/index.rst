Guidance Computer Software Requirements (Luminary)
==================================================

Luminary is the lunar module guidance computer's (LGC's) flight software:
the programs that fly the descent (P63 braking, P64 approach, P65 and P66
landing, P68 landing confirmed, P70 and P71 aborts), navigation, the
display and keyboard (DSKY), and the executive that shares the computer
between them. Each mission's own numbers come from its erasable load.
