Block reference
===============

.. rst-class:: lead

   Every BuildingBlock scripts can call, documented by the block itself:
   how to call it, and what it does.

Each entry is generated from the block: the calls come from its ``usage()``
and the text from its docstring (see :ref:`documenting-blocks`). In a usage,
``name?`` is optional and ``name...`` or ``name ...`` repeats.
