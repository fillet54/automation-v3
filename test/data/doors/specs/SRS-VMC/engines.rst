Engines
=======

.. requirement:: VMCENG00001
   :satisfies: SEG-AIR-003
   :subsystem: ENG
   :verification: Test
   :last-modified-by: doors-import

   The software shall provide real-time monitoring and control of engine
   parameters, such as thrust, temperature, and fuel consumption.

.. requirement:: VMCENG00002
   :satisfies: SEG-AIR-003
   :subsystem: ENG
   :verification: Test
   :last-modified-by: doors-import

   The software shall have automatic fault detection and recovery
   capabilities for engine-related systems.

.. requirement:: VMCENG00003
   :satisfies: SEG-AIR-003
   :subsystem: ENG
   :verification: Test
   :last-modified-by: doors-import

   The software shall support multiple engine types and configurations,
   including turbojets, turbofans, and turboprops.

.. requirement:: VMCENG00004
   :satisfies: SEG-AIR-003
   :subsystem: ENG
   :verification: Test
   :last-modified-by: doors-import

   The software shall provide engine performance optimization based on
   environmental conditions and mission requirements.

.. requirement:: VMCENG00005
   :satisfies: SEG-AIR-003
   :subsystem: ENG
   :verification: Test
   :last-modified-by: doors-import

   efficient fuel consumption and usage.

.. requirement:: VMCENG00006
   :satisfies: SEG-AIR-003
   :subsystem: ENG
   :verification: Test
   :last-modified-by: doors-import

   and perform engine tests.

.. requirement:: VMCENG00007
   :satisfies: SEG-AIR-003
   :subsystem: ENG
   :verification: Test
   :last-modified-by: doors-import

   The software shall provide real-time monitoring of engine health and
   maintenance requirements.

.. requirement:: VMCENG00008
   :satisfies: SEG-AIR-003

   standards, such as JAR-E.
