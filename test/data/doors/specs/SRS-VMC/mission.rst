Mission planning
================

.. requirement:: VMCMP00001
   :satisfies: SEG-AIR-002
   :subsystem: MP
   :verification: Test
   :last-modified-by: doors-import

   The software shall provide a user-friendly interface for mission
   planning and execution.

.. requirement:: VMCMP00002
   :satisfies: SEG-AIR-002
   :subsystem: MP
   :verification: Test
   :last-modified-by: doors-import

   The software shall allow for flexible and configurable mission planning,
   including route definition, waypoint management, and payload operations.

.. requirement:: VMCMP00003
   :satisfies: SEG-AIR-002
   :subsystem: MP
   :verification: Test
   :last-modified-by: doors-import

   The software shall have the ability to import and export mission plans
   in a standard format.

.. requirement:: VMCMP00004
   :satisfies: SEG-AIR-002
   :subsystem: MP
   :verification: Test
   :last-modified-by: doors-import

   The software shall provide real-time monitoring of mission progress and
   the ability to modify plans in-flight.

.. requirement:: VMCMP00005
   :satisfies: SEG-AIR-002
   :subsystem: MP
   :verification: Test
   :last-modified-by: doors-import

   The software shall provide automatic mission optimization based on
   vehicle constraints and environment conditions.

.. requirement:: VMCMP00006
   :satisfies: SEG-AIR-002
   :subsystem: MP
   :verification: Test
   :last-modified-by: doors-import

   The software shall have a comprehensive library of pre-defined missions
   and scenarios for rapid and efficient planning.

.. requirement:: VMCMP00007
   :satisfies: SEG-AIR-002
   :subsystem: MP
   :verification: Test
   :last-modified-by: doors-import

   The software shall provide mission simulation capabilities for pre-
   flight testing and analysis.

.. requirement:: VMCMP00008
   :satisfies: SEG-AIR-002
   :subsystem: MP
   :verification: Test
   :last-modified-by: doors-import

   The software shall support integration with other onboard systems, such
   as sensors and instruments, for comprehensive mission planning and
   execution.
