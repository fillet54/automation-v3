Communication
=============

.. requirement:: VMCCOMM00001
   :satisfies: SEG-AIR-001
   :subsystem: COMM
   :verification: Test
   :last-modified-by: doors-import

   The software shall provide secure and reliable communication between the
   vehicle and ground control.

.. requirement:: VMCCOMM00002
   :satisfies: SEG-AIR-001
   :subsystem: COMM
   :verification: Test
   :last-modified-by: doors-import

   The software shall support multiple communication protocols, including
   telemetry and command.

.. requirement:: VMCCOMM00003
   :satisfies: SEG-AIR-001
   :subsystem: COMM
   :verification: Test
   :last-modified-by: doors-import

   The software shall provide real-time monitoring of communication links
   and automatic recovery in case of failures.

.. requirement:: VMCCOMM00004
   :satisfies: SEG-AIR-001
   :subsystem: COMM
   :verification: Test
   :last-modified-by: doors-import

   The software shall support data compression and encryption to ensure
   secure transmission of sensitive information.

.. requirement:: VMCCOMM00005
   :satisfies: SEG-AIR-001
   :subsystem: COMM
   :verification: Test
   :last-modified-by: doors-import

   The software shall have a configurable and adaptive communication
   strategy to support different missions and environments.

.. requirement:: VMCCOMM00006
   :satisfies: SEG-AIR-001
   :subsystem: COMM
   :verification: Test
   :last-modified-by: doors-import

   The software shall support communication redundancy and fault tolerance
   to ensure mission continuity in case of failures.

.. requirement:: VMCCOMM00007
   :satisfies: SEG-AIR-001
   :subsystem: COMM
   :verification: Test
   :last-modified-by: doors-import

   The software shall provide reliable and efficient data transfer and
   storage for post-mission analysis.

.. requirement:: VMCCOMM00008
   :satisfies: SEG-AIR-001
   :subsystem: COMM
   :verification: Test
   :last-modified-by: doors-import

   standards and regulations.
