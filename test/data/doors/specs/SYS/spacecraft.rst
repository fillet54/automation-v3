Spacecraft operations
=====================

.. requirement:: SYS-001
   :verification: Analysis

   The mission system shall provide Earth observation imagery of any point between 60° N and 60° S within 48 h of a tasking request.

.. requirement:: SYS-002
   :verification: Analysis

   The mission system shall let operators command every flight element and receive its telemetry, with every command and every telemetry packet authenticated.

.. requirement:: SYS-004
   :verification: Analysis

   The space segment shall sustain its operations through every eclipse of a 500 km sun-synchronous orbit.
