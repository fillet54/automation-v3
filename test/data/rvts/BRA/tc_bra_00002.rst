=========================
Brake Pressure Over Limit
=========================
Applies 95 against the brake limit of 80.

.. warning::

   Fails on purpose: 95 is over the brake limit, so the brakes-hold
   defblock fails and VMCBRA00007 rolls up Red. Use it to see a failing
   step and the nested calls that explain it.

Requirements
------------
1. :req:`VMCBRA00007`

.. rvt::

   (Precondition "Demo running in normal mode"
     (demo-in-mode? :normal)
     :heal (start-demo :normal))

Steps
-----

.. rvt::

   (brakes-hold 95)
